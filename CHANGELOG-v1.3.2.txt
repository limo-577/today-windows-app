Today v1.3.2

- Added per-task Start/Pause controls directly inside the independent floating window.
- Clicking Play on a task starts that exact task timer.
- The active running task shows Pause; when paused it shows Play to resume.
- Switching to another task from the floating window correctly finalizes the previous focus session before starting the new one.
- Completed tasks do not show a timer button.
- Main window and floating window remain synchronized through Electron IPC.
